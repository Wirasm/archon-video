# Kind: social

A general-interest short that earns attention with an insight, not a product.

- **Voice.** Third person or plain second person, essayistic, conversational. The narrator is a sharp friend who noticed something, not a presenter.
- **Promise.** One idea the viewer did not have before, paid off inside the video. A reframe, a surprising mechanism, a number that changes how something looks.
- **Specifics.** Names, numbers, places and moments beat general claims. If a claim needs a source to be believed, say where it comes from in plain words or leave it out.
- **No call to action.** No "follow for more", no "link in bio". The ending should make the opening line land again, so the loop feels deliberate.
- **Pictures.** Real places, people, objects and actions that exist on film. Every shot should show what is being said at that moment.
