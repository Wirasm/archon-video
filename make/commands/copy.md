The hardest rule: only promise what the narration delivers. A description offering "5 ways to fix it" for a video that contains no five ways is a lie that costs the retention it buys. If the narration is reflective rather than practical, sell the insight, not a checklist.

Within that, write for search and for a reason to care. Lead with the value or the tension, not a summary. Use the words a person would type into a search box. No engagement bait ("comment below!", "you won't believe"). Sound like the brand's voice.

- youtube_title: at most 70 characters, searchable.
- youtube_description: 2-3 short paragraphs; the first line carries the hook because it is all that shows before "more". End with 3-5 plain search phrases on their own lines.
- youtube_tags: 8-12 lowercase tags, no "#".
- tiktok_caption: at most 150 characters including hashtags, conversational.
- instagram_caption: 1-2 sentences plus a line of hashtags.
- hashtags: 6-10, mixed broad and niche, without the "#".
- alt_text: one sentence describing the visuals for accessibility.
